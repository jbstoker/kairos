# Join the Kairos Community

**Time you observe, not time you obey.**

Kairos is a living system — and it grows best when it is shared.

---

## How to Contribute

### 1. Share Your Moment
- Take a photo.
- Add your Kairos date (e.g., `14:32 · Bloom · Harvest Moon 4 · Radiance · 4.54B / 2026.622`).
- Share it on social media with the hashtag `#KairosTime`.
- Tag us (if we have a social account) so we can see and celebrate with you.

### 2. Add Your Knowledge
- **Plants**: Do you know a plant that grows in your region? Add it to the seasonal data.
- **Festivals**: Do you celebrate a festival that isn't listed? Add it.
- **Traditions**: Do you follow a tradition that we don't have? Share it.

### 3. Observe Together
- Compare your solar noon with others in your region.
- See how the moon phase changes across different parts of the world.
- Share your observations and learn from others.

### 4. Help Us Grow
- **Report bugs**: If something isn't working, let us know.
- **Suggest features**: What would make Kairos better for you?
- **Share your story**: How has Kairos changed your relationship with time?

---

## Community Guidelines
