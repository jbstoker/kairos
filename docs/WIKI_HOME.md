## The Kairos Format

`19:33 (293.4°) · ⛲Well · Harvest Moon 9 · ☀️Radiance · 4.54B / 2026.635`

The heart of the web app is the **sky-dome**: the Sun and Moon are placed by
their real altitude and azimuth on a circular degree wheel (0–360°) with
visible altitude rings (20/40/60/80°) — below the horizon a bead becomes a
dimmed ghost on the wheel edge, a soft twilight glow fades in around the
horizon at dusk/dawn (civil −6…0°, nautical −12…−6°), and a sunrise
countdown appears while the sun is below it. Real solar eclipses light up as
overlapping beads with a `🌑 ECLIPSE IN PROGRESS` line. The header reads
**true solar time** (12:00 = solar noon) with the Sun's azimuth as the
degree — so the number and the bead always agree. See the [User
Guide](GUIDE.md) for how to read it and how to set your location.
